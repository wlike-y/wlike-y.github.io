I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology, expecting to graduate in June 2027. I have been admitted through recommendation-based graduate admission to the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences), where I expect to enroll in September 2027 in Computer Application Technology.

My research training so far has centered on the security of cyber-physical systems, in particular false data injection attacks and resilient security control in multi-area power systems, where I worked on virtual-network-based defense methods. My current interests lie in network data analysis and mining, cyberspace asset identification and knowledge graph construction, and the use of large language models and graph-enhanced retrieval for network data analysis.

## Research Interests
{: #research-interests}

* Deep analysis and mining of network data
* Cyberspace asset identification and knowledge graph construction
* Large language models for network data analysis
* Graph-enhanced retrieval and knowledge-augmented analysis

These are directions I am currently exploring. The completed research described below is in a different area, and no results in the directions above have been published yet.

## Research Experience
{: #research-experience}

**Smart-Grid Security and FDIA Defense**
{: .entry-title}

- Original project title: 基于网络攻击的智能电网态势感知与协同防御问题研究
- Program: Jiangsu Provincial Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program
- Status: completed (project closed)
- Research problem: false data injection attacks (FDIA) and resilient security control in multi-area power systems, where measurements can be manipulated across interconnected areas and conventional state estimation may be silently corrupted.
- Methods: virtual-network-based defense, electrical and structural criticality assessment, topology optimization, and physical–virtual network coupling.

Confirmed outputs:

1. National invention patent, authorized, first inventor: 一种基于虚拟网络的多区域电力系统防御方法 (English rendering, not an official title: "A Virtual Network-Based Defense Method for Multi-Area Power Systems").
2. First-author manuscript: *Virtual Network-Based Resilient Security Control for Multi-Area Power Systems under False Data Injection Attacks*. The project is completed, but the manuscript is still in the review process; see [Publications](#publications).
